About the Traveller

👤 The Day Job & Beyond

Senior Consultant at Deloitte USI. Work pays the bills; travel makes the bills worth paying. Currently on a mission to get fitter — because 4000 km bike trips don't ride themselves.

❤️ Things That Light Me Up

Literature, poetry & shayari · Fictional novels that transport you · Helping people (genuinely, not performatively) · Long bike rides with no fixed destination · Understanding art · Board games with stakes · Hanging out with friends who make time stop · Meeting new, interesting humans · Learning something I didn't know yesterday · And obviously — travelling.

🚫 Things That Don't Sit Well

Manipulation disguised as friendship · Having nothing fun to look forward to · Watching someone around me be sad · People who disrespect the places they visit · A lack of empathy, civic sense, or basic respect · Selfishness that comes at others' cost — especially on group trips.

What I Bring to the Table

🏔️ Battle-Tested — You Won't Have to Babysit Me

First solo trip: Himachal, December, no thermals. Rookie move — but I survived, adapted, came back sharper. Every trip since has been meticulously researched. I'll handle whatever goes sideways.

🏍️ Endurance-Proven — I Won't Slow Things Down

4000+ km on a motorcycle in 21 days. Heat, broken roads, fatigue, mechanical breakdowns — didn't stop once. I'm the guy saying, "Chal, next stop kitna dur hai?"

🤝 Socially Effortless — No Awkward Phase Required

I've walked into circles of strangers at 12,000 feet and walked out with friendships that still hold. Zero ego, zero conflict, easy dynamics.

🌿 Respectful — I Won't Get Your Group into Trouble

Full fun, zero controversies. I keep things entertaining without crossing lines. The organiser can genuinely relax.

🎭 The Vibe Architect — Nobody Stays Bored

Funny most of the time. Playlists ready. Stories and jokes to fill any stretch. Free entertainment system that carries its own backpack.

TL;DR: Low-maintenance, high-energy, socially smooth. Probably everyone's favourite travel buddy by day three. Zero drama guaranteed.
